# InvolvEd

**Turn involvement into opportunity.**

An AI-powered student engagement and professional development platform designed to bridge the gap between extracurricular involvement and career readiness.

[Explore InvolvEd](https://involv-ed.vercel.app/)




## The Problem

College students develop valuable professional skills through student organizations, leadership positions, volunteer work, and extracurricular projects. However, these experiences are often difficult to quantify, document, and communicate to potential employers.

This creates a disconnect between what students accomplish outside the classroom and how their competencies are recognized in the workforce.

## The Solution

InvolvEd aims to transform extracurricular experiences into measurable, career-relevant competencies through a centralized digital platform that connects students, student organizations, and faculty advisors.

The platform brings together organizational management, professional development, and artificial intelligence to help students recognize the value of their involvement.

## Key Features


### Student Experience

- Professional skill tracking
- Extracurricular involvement records
- Digital professional portfolios
- Resume bullet generation
- Career exploration and internship matching

### Organization Management

- Project and task management
- Member coordination
- Skill-based task recommendations
- Event attendance tracking
- Organizational performance insights

### Faculty and Advisor Experience

- Student organization oversight
- Activity and progress monitoring
- Competency verification
- Organizational collaboration

## Invo AI

Invo AI is an integrated artificial intelligence assistant designed to help students identify their strengths, communicate their accomplishments, and explore professional opportunities.

Its capabilities include:

- Personalized career guidance
- AI-assisted resume development
- Professional competency identification
- Activity and organizational analysis
- Career development recommendations
